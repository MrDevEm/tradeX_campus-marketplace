How tradeX Works

Five straightforward steps designed exclusively for university students to exchange goods safely on campus.

01

Create Account

Sign up in under 60 seconds with your student name, official university email, and academic department.

02

Verify Student ID

Confirm your campus email with a quick OTP and upload your student ID to earn the verified peer badge.

03

Discover or Post

Browse campus deals or post your textbook or gear with photos, price in ETB, and preferred meeting spot.

04

Chat In-App

Directly message sellers inside tradeX. Negotiate price, agree on swap items, or confirm availability.

05

Campus Handover

Meet in high-visibility campus spots (library, student center, or café) to inspect the item and finalize.
